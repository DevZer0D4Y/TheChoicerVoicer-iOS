# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for The Choicer Voicer versions made with Godot 4.0 to 4.4 (tested with 0.5.1).

- Runs the game from your own Windows `.exe` or `.pck` in the app's folder in the Files app; the app contains no part of the game.
- Microphone input works on iOS, with the microphone permission requested at launch.
- Full screen on every iPhone and iPad, in either landscape orientation. Dub mode's TV and remote keep their PC layout, and pack videos keep their proportions.
- Two-finger tap as right click.
- The game's "open folder" buttons open the folder in the Files app.
- Packs, recordings and saves in the app's folder in the Files app.
